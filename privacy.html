3. Data We Collect

StudyLaptop.com itself does not require user registration and does not intentionally collect sensitive personal data.

The following information may be processed:

  • Email address (only if voluntarily submitted through the newsletter form)
  • Anonymous analytics data
  • Technical server information (e.g. browser type, IP logs, access times)

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Data processing is carried out in accordance with Art. 6 GDPR.

  • Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR — user consent for newsletter subscriptions
  • Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR — legitimate interest in improving website performance and security

5. Cookies

StudyLaptop.com does not use tracking cookies or marketing cookies.

Technically necessary cookies may be used for basic website functionality.

6. Analytics

This website uses Plausible Analytics, a privacy-friendly analytics service.

Plausible does not use cookies and does not collect personally identifiable information.

All analytics data is processed anonymously and in aggregated form only.

More information: https://plausible.io/data-policy

7. Newsletter

If you voluntarily subscribe to the newsletter, your email address will only be used to send updates, recommendations, and student laptop deals.

You can unsubscribe at any time via the unsubscribe link contained in every email.

Newsletter data is processed exclusively for this purpose and will not be sold or shared for marketing purposes.

8. Affiliate Links

StudyLaptop.com participates in affiliate programs.

Some links on this website may be affiliate links. If users purchase products through such links, we may earn a commission at no additional cost to the user.

9. Hosting and Server Logs

The website is hosted by a third-party hosting provider.

For technical and security reasons, the hosting provider may automatically collect server log data, such as:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Operating system
  • Access time
  • Requested pages

This data is used exclusively to ensure the stability and security of the website.

10. Data Retention

Personal data is stored only as long as necessary for the respective purpose.

  • Newsletter data: until unsubscribed
  • Technical logs: limited retention for security purposes
  • Anonymous analytics data: aggregated only

11. Your Rights Under GDPR

Under the GDPR, users have the right to:

  • Request access to stored personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of personal data
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • File a complaint with a data protection authority
