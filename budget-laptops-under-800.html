What to expect under €800

A good student laptop under €800 should usually include SSD storage, at least 8GB RAM, a comfortable keyboard, and enough battery life for a school or university day.

Who should buy a budget laptop?

Budget laptops are best for students who mainly use Google Docs, Microsoft Office, web browsing, video calls, learning platforms, and light programming.

Final recommendation

For most students, the best budget laptop is the one that balances price, battery life, portability, and reliability. Avoid very cheap models with slow storage or too little memory.
