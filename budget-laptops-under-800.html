Frequently Asked Questions

Is €800 enough for a good student laptop?

Yes. €800 is enough to buy a reliable student laptop with SSD storage, good battery life, and enough performance for university work, research, and everyday productivity.

How much RAM should a laptop under €800 have?

Ideally, students should choose 16GB RAM. However, 8GB RAM can still work for basic tasks such as note-taking, web browsing, and document editing.

Can I get a good laptop for college under €800?

Absolutely. Many laptops under €800 provide excellent value and are more than powerful enough for most college majors, including business, education, and general studies.

Are Chromebooks good for university students?

Chromebooks can be a good choice for students who mainly use web-based tools like Google Docs and online learning platforms. However, some university software may require Windows or macOS.

What should I avoid when buying a budget laptop?

Avoid laptops with slow storage, very old processors, poor battery life, or limited upgrade options. Spending a little more often provides much better long-term value.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get a good student laptop under €800?

Yes. Many excellent student laptops are available under €800, including models from Lenovo, ASUS, Acer, HP, and Dell. The key is choosing a laptop with at least 16GB RAM, an SSD, and a modern processor.

Is €800 enough for university?

For most students, €800 is enough to buy a reliable laptop for note-taking, research, online classes, programming, and general university work.

Should students choose 8GB or 16GB RAM?

While 8GB RAM can handle basic tasks, 16GB RAM is recommended for smoother multitasking and better long-term usability.

What processor should a student laptop have?

Students should look for modern Intel Core 5, Intel Core 7, AMD Ryzen 5, AMD Ryzen 7, or Apple Silicon processors for the best balance of performance and battery life.

How long should a budget student laptop last?

A quality budget laptop should last four to six years for most students if it has enough RAM, SSD storage, and a modern processor.
