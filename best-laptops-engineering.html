What specs do engineering students need?

For engineering, aim for at least 16GB RAM, a modern Intel Core, AMD Ryzen, or Apple Silicon processor, and SSD storage. Students using CAD or 3D software should check whether their software requires Windows or a dedicated graphics card.

Windows or macOS for engineering?

Many engineering applications are Windows-first, so Windows laptops are often the safest choice. macOS can still be excellent for programming, research, writing, and general study work, but always check your university software requirements.

Final recommendation

If you want the safest all-round choice, choose a Windows laptop such as a ThinkPad or Dell XPS. If your work is mostly coding, writing, and research, a MacBook Air can also be a strong option.
