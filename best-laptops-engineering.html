What specs do engineering students need?

For engineering, aim for at least 16GB RAM, a modern Intel Core, AMD Ryzen, or Apple Silicon processor, fast SSD storage, and enough graphics performance for your software. If your budget is limited, start with our Best Student Laptops Under €800 guide if you're looking for the best value before investing in a higher-performance engineering laptop.

Windows or macOS for engineering?

Many engineering applications are Windows-first, so Windows laptops are often the safest choice. macOS can still be excellent for programming, research, writing, and general study work, but always check your university software requirements.

Final recommendation

If you want the safest all-round choice, choose a Windows laptop such as a ThinkPad or Dell XPS. If your work is mostly coding, writing, and research, a MacBook Air can also be a strong option. Frequently Asked Questions

What specs do engineering students need in a laptop?

Engineering students should usually look for at least 16GB RAM, SSD storage, a modern Intel Core or AMD Ryzen processor, and strong Windows compatibility for engineering software.

Is 16GB RAM enough for engineering students?

Yes, 16GB RAM is enough for most engineering students. Students using heavy CAD, simulations, or large projects may benefit from 32GB RAM.

Is MacBook good for engineering students?

A MacBook can be good for coding, writing, research, and general study work. However, many engineering programs are Windows-first, so students should check their university software requirements before choosing macOS.

Do engineering students need a gaming laptop?

Not always. A gaming laptop can help if you need dedicated graphics for CAD, 3D work, or simulations, but many engineering students can use a strong business laptop or ultrabook.

What is the best laptop size for engineering students?

A 14-inch or 16-inch laptop is usually best. A 14-inch laptop is easier to carry, while a 16-inch laptop gives more screen space for CAD, coding, and technical work.
