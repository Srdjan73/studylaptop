MacBook vs Windows for Students 2026

Choosing between a MacBook and a Windows laptop is one of the biggest decisions students make before starting university. The right choice depends on your major, software requirements, budget, and how you plan to use your laptop. This guide compares both platforms to help you choose with confidence.

Quick Comparison

Category MacBook Windows Laptop
Best for Battery life, portability, long-term value Budget choice, software flexibility, gaming
Price Usually higher More budget options
Software Great for general study, design, writing Best for Windows-only university software
Battery Excellent Depends on model

Choose MacBook If...

  • You want long battery life
  • You prefer a quiet, lightweight laptop
  • You mainly write, research, browse, and attend online classes
  • You want strong long-term value
  • Your course does not require Windows-only software

Choose Windows If...

  • You need specific Windows software
  • You want more budget choices
  • You study engineering , IT, or other technical subjects
  • You want gaming or stronger GPU options
  • You prefer more ports and hardware variety

Choose Based on Your Major

Your major matters more than the logo on the laptop. Before choosing between a MacBook and a Windows laptop, check whether your university requires software that only runs well on one platform.

Engineering and Architecture

Windows is usually the safer choice because many courses use software such as SolidWorks, Revit, AutoCAD, and specialised simulation tools. Always check your course requirements before choosing a MacBook.

Computer Science and Programming

Both platforms can work well. MacBooks are popular for coding, battery life, and Unix-based development, while Windows offers broader software compatibility, gaming support, and more hardware choices.

Business, Law, and Humanities

Either platform is suitable for writing, research, presentations, spreadsheets, and online classes. Choose based on your budget, portability needs, and preferred operating system. If you're shopping on a budget, check our Best Student Laptops Under €800 guide.

Design, Media, and Creative Studies

MacBooks are a strong choice for creative work, especially for students who value battery life, display quality, and the Apple ecosystem. Windows may be better for 3D work, gaming-related courses, or software that benefits from a dedicated graphics card.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is MacBook or Windows better for students?

MacBook is usually better for battery life, portability, and long-term reliability. Windows is better for students who need specific software, gaming, engineering tools, or a lower price.

Are MacBooks worth it for college students?

Yes, MacBooks can be worth it for students who want excellent battery life, quiet performance, strong build quality, and a laptop that can last several years.

Which students should choose Windows?

Windows is usually better for engineering, IT, architecture, gaming, and courses that require Windows-only software.

Can engineering students use a MacBook?

Some engineering students can use a MacBook, especially for coding and general study work. However, many CAD and engineering programs work better on Windows, so students should check their course software requirements first.

Is Windows cheaper than MacBook for students?

Yes. Windows laptops usually offer more budget options, including many good student laptops under €800.
